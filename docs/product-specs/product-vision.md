# Product vision

## Purpose

Make books and other text documents more enjoyable to ingest by turning them into dynamic, lecture-like audiovisual experiences without sacrificing their content.

## User outcome

A user provides a text-based document and receives a coherent movie with:

- complete, engaging spoken narration;
- relevant supporting visuals;
- pacing and transitions suitable for long-form listening;
- a reliable way to trace the output back to the source.

## Product promises

- The output preserves the source's information and meaning.
- Presentation may be adapted for listening, but content is not silently summarized away.
- Visuals clarify, contextualize, or maintain attention; they do not replace required narration.
- Long jobs are observable, resumable, and reviewable.
- Users can understand what was generated, what source passage it represents, and where corrections are needed.

## Non-goals for the first product slice

- Replacing the author's ideas with a shortened interpretation.
- Producing unrelated decorative video at the expense of comprehension.
- Hiding model uncertainty, failed extraction, skipped passages, or generation errors.
- Solving every document format, language, voice style, and rendering target at once.

## First-slice success criteria

The first executable slice should accept one well-defined document format and produce a short end-to-end preview containing source-linked narration, synchronized visuals, and a completeness report.
